## Notes for re-generation

- Keep the seed and change one clause at a time to hold the look while varying
  wardrobe, angle, or cast.
- The model renders on-screen text unreliably. Prompts deliberately say "an AI
  chat interface" rather than naming or spelling out a product UI — keep it that
  way, and keep screens small in frame or angled away.
- If a frame comes back cool or over-lit, add "warm golden hour interior light,
  natural contrast, no studio strobes" before re-rolling.
