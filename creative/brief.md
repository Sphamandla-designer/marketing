# Creative Brief — AI Training Workshop

## Subject

A South African marketing consultant attending a premium AI training workshop.
Working with ChatGPT and Claude on a laptop while discussing ideas with other
professionals around a shared table.

## Setting

Modern conference venue. Warm natural lighting from large windows. Premium
furniture — walnut and oak tables, designer chairs, plants, minimal
architecture. Printed workbooks, branded notebooks, pens, coffee on the table.

## Treatment

- **Genre:** documentary / editorial reportage, not corporate stock
- **Expressions:** authentic — mid-sentence, listening, concentrating, laughing.
  Nobody grinning at the lens.
- **Interaction:** realistic. People lean in, gesture, share a screen, annotate
  a workbook. The room is working, not posing.
- **Wardrobe:** business casual. Linen blazers, rolled sleeves, simple tops.
- **Optics:** shallow depth of field, creamy background bokeh. 35mm for the
  environmental wide, 50mm for the table shot, 85mm for portraits and
  over-the-shoulder.
- **Grade:** warm and natural. Golden afternoon light. Realistic skin texture,
  no plastic retouching.

## Casting

Diverse and locally credible — the room should read as a South African
professional audience, mixed ethnicities and ages, majority Black professionals
in lead frames.

## What to avoid

- Cold blue "AI / tech" colour grading
- Floating UI, glowing holograms, circuit-board motifs
- Legible on-screen text or brand logos (renders unreliably and dates fast)
- Empty conference-room stock clichés — handshakes, pointing at blank charts
- Over-lit, shadowless studio lighting
